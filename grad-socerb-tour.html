Grad Socerb

Grad Socerb stands on the edge of a limestone cliff, high above the Gulf of Trieste, just across the border in Slovenia. Few visitors find it, and it gives one of the widest views of the whole region.

It is the finale of our day. We time the tour to reach the hill before sunset, so you watch the light change over the city, the sea and the two coasts.

What to expect

  • Sunset views over Trieste and the gulf
  • Just across the border: bring an ID card or passport
  • The finale of every tour
