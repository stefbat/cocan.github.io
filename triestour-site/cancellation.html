Payment and cancellation policy

Payment and free cancellation (up to 48 hours)

100% payment is required upfront to secure your seats. Full refund if canceled at least 48 hours before the tour start time.

Changes within 48 hours of the tour

Bookings made or canceled within 48 hours of the tour are non-refundable. If your plans change unexpectedly, contact us and we will happily reschedule your tour for free (subject to seat availability).

Weather or organizer cancellations

Tours run rain or shine. Triestour reserves the right to cancel a tour at any time, usually due to severe weather, illness, or emergencies. In such cases, you get a 100% refund or a free reschedule.

Late arrivals and no-shows

No refunds are given for late arrivals, no-shows, or once the tour officially starts. If future seats are free, we may arrange a reschedule (subject to seat availability).
