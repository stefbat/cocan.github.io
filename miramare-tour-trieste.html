Miramare Castle

Miramare is the white castle that seems to float on the Adriatic, a few kilometres from Trieste. It was built in the 1850s for Archduke Maximilian of Habsburg, who later became emperor of Mexico, and he planned both the house and its gardens himself.

We tell the story of the castle and its builder while you walk the seaside park, and we choose the hour when the light on the water is softest and the crowds are thin.

What to expect

  • Small group, never more than 8 guests
  • Best light and fewer visitors by choosing the time of day
  • The castle interior has a separate ticket if you wish to enter
